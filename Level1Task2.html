About US

Welcome to Go2Africa, Part of the Nawiri Group

For over 25 years, we've been crafting and delivering personalised, Africa-based safaris, shaped by our first-hand knowledge and deep local connections. Since 1998, more than 200,000 travellers have trusted us to bring their travel dreams to life — earning the trust of those who return year after year.

Our deep roots in Africa mean we have invaluable expertise about every destination, every lodge, every experience, and every hidden gem we recommend. We don't rely on second-hand information — we go, we experience, and we build lasting relationships to ensure your adventure is seamless, immersive, and unforgettable.

Recognised by Condé Nast Traveler, Travel + Leisure, and the World Travel Awards, we take pride in being among the world's best. But more than accolades, it's the voices of our travellers that matter most — our 2,000+ five-star reviews on Feefo are a testament to the quality we consistently deliver.

From your first enquiry with us until you return home safely, we're by your side, handling every detail with 24/7 support. And because of the hard-earned trust we've built with our on-the-ground partners, we know they'll go above and beyond for you, just as we do.

A destination alone doesn't make for an incredible adventure — it's the details that matter. It's in the details that makes going to Africa with us your greatest adventure.
